Who I Am

About

I build software, design the interfaces around it, and care about how the two feel together.

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of British Columbia Okanagan, working across full-stack development, AI-powered systems, and data analytics — and across Figma, Webflow, and layout design on the side. Most of what I build lives at the point where those two skill sets meet.

I like projects that force me to learn something new, and I care as much about how an interface reads as I do about what's running underneath it. Outside of that: football, tennis, and editing video.
